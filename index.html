⚠️ Important Limitations

  • Automated analysis can produce false positives and false negatives.
  • Results are based on technical signal inspection only — not validated ML model inference.
  • Compressed, resized, or re-encoded media may obscure or introduce signals.
  • Metadata can be stripped, forged, or absent — absence is not proof of manipulation.
  • This tool is a first-pass inspection aid, not a definitive authenticator.
  • Never rely solely on this tool for high-stakes decisions.

How it works

01

Upload & Validate

Your file is read locally. Format, size, and content type are checked before analysis begins.

02

Signal Inspection

Depending on media type, the app inspects metadata, pixel statistics, frequency patterns, and consistency signals.

03

Cautious Assessment

Results are presented with an uncertainty range. No media is declared definitively real or fake.

04

Guided Verification

Actionable next steps help you decide when further human review or specialist tools are needed.

Privacy & Security

🔒

100% local processing

All analysis happens in your browser. Your file never leaves your device.

🗑️

Immediate deletion

Files are held in memory only during analysis. Clearing or closing the tab removes all data.

🚫

No third-party uploads

Nothing is sent to external APIs or cloud services without your explicit consent.

🛡️

Content validation

File content is validated beyond the extension to reduce malformed-file risks.
